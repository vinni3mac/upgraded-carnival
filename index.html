About

Vincent Mano is a Woodland Hills, California–based musician whose career spans multiple roles in the music industry. Performing and writing since the early 2000s, he first gained recognition as the lead singer of Fallover Tuesday and has collaborated with artists such as Cory Carson and Gardner Cole.

A versatile multi-instrumentalist, Vincent blends alternative rock with electronic-powered pop, crafting layered, genre-crossing melodies that are now gaining significant traction. His current album, "Polished Artifacts," is available on all streaming platforms, and he is preparing for a new album and tour with a release set for 2026.
